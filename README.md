## What it does

Spare Price Scout continuously compares spare-parts prices across OEMs, distributors, and aftermarket suppliers, alerting you when a part you buy is available cheaper — typical savings of 10–25% on MRO spend.

## Key features

- **Cross-supplier comparison** — OEM vs aftermarket vs remanufactured, side by side.
- **Price-drop alerts** — notifications when watched parts fall below target price.
- **Equivalent-part matching** — AI finds compatible alternatives by spec and part number.
- **Spend analysis** — your top overpriced SKUs ranked by savings opportunity.
- **Supplier scorecards** — price, lead time, and reliability per vendor.

## Who it's for

Maintenance, procurement, and MRO teams in manufacturing, fleets, and facilities.
