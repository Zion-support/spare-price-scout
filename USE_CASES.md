# Use Cases — Spare Price Scout

[Live app](https://ziontechgroup.com/spare-price-scout/) · [Plans](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/plans/) · [Discovery call](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/)

## 1. Plant MRO spend
A manufacturer benchmarks its top 500 SKUs and finds 18% average savings, switching 60 SKUs to qualified aftermarket suppliers.

## 2. Fleet parts purchasing
Brake and filter categories compared across distributors; savings compound with fuel savings from [Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/).

## 3. Emergency breakdown sourcing
During an unplanned stop, equivalent-part matching finds an in-stock alternative 40% cheaper with same-day delivery.

## 4. Supplier negotiation
Price-history evidence feeds renewals tracked in [Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/).

## 5. Invoice verification
Scouted target prices are checked against actual invoices via [Invoice Match AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/invoice-match-ai/) — savings are verified, not assumed.
